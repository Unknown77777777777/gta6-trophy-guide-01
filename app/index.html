    Roadmap to the platinum

    PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 difficulty, about 200 hours, in a single playthrough with no online trophies. The story takes roughly 60–80 hours; after the credits, free-roam continues and everything needed for 100% completion is still reachable. There is no mission replay, and cheats disable trophies and saving — keep cheats off for the whole run.

    Only 1 potentially missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — the first meditation is offered at Jason's house in Chapter 1 and becomes unavailable after that chapter (PowerPyx / GTABase). Do it the moment the game offers it.
